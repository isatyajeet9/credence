Photosynthesis is essentially a biological process where photosynthetic organisms—like trees, algae, and some bacteria—take light energy from the sun and convert it into chemical energy. This energy gets stored inside carbohydrates (like sugar) which they build from water and carbon dioxide. Usually, oxygen is let out as a byproduct.

Many scientists warn that if carbon emissions continue to rise, the global rate of photosynthesis could be severely impacted by extreme climate change.
